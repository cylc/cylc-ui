The log view now supports two viewing modes: **HEAD** and **TAIL**.

Logs are limited to `MAX LINES`, which defaults to 5000 and can be configured by users. When a log exceeds this limit, only the first or last `MAX LINES` lines are displayed, depending on the selected viewing mode.

**HEAD mode**
- Displays the beginning of the log.
- When viewing a running job or workflow, new lines are displayed as they are added, until `MAX LINES` is reached. Any subsequent lines are truncated.

**TAIL mode**
- Displays the end of the log.
- When a log is shorter than `MAX LINES` when opened, the entire log is displayed, including any new lines added while the job or workflow is running.
- A log that grows past `MAX LINES` is NOT truncated until the viewer is refreshed.
- When a log already exceeds `MAX LINES` when opened or refreshed, the beginning of the log is truncated to show only the last `MAX LINES` lines.

Users can change their `MAX LINES` setting to view more or less of a log, either to locate specific content or to improve performance.

Users can also switch between HEAD and TAIL modes directly from the log view. When a log is truncated, scrolling to the relevant end of the displayed content reveals a warning banner with a button to load the truncated portion.
